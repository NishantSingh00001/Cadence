A QUICK FIELD NOTE

Why the four chords?

Progressions often feel like a journey because harmony creates expectation and release. In a major key, I–V–vi–IV is familiar and open; vi–IV–I–V begins in a more reflective place. There are no rules to obey—just useful starting points.
